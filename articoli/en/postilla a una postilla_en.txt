The article from December 12, 2025 is certainly already outdated. The two models — heavy industrial capitalism of hyper-production and the service capitalism of mediated disintermediation — are in fact integrated. On Vinted, in fact, a huge share of "used" clothes are actually Shein clothes, bought and resold on Vinted at three times the price.

Online guides explain to digital proletarians how to exploit this system to become small entrepreneurs (that is: doing online commerce that mostly enriches the platforms).

In this sense the miracle is complete. Violent industrial production (Shein) gets re-virginized by impoverished Westerners in order to become "vintage" and free everyone — us, Shein, society — from any guilt.
